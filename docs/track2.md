# Track2: unseen generalisation track

Every policy and the shield were developed on `maps/Singapore`. Track2 is a second track that no policy has seen,
used only to test whether they generalise. Image: [`maps/Track2/Track2_map.png`](../maps/Track2/Track2_map.png).

## The track

Generated by `racing/track_gen.py` (`VERTICES_TRACK2`), same format as Singapore: 2.8 m wide (1.4 m half width in
the centerline CSV), 0.05 m/px, counter-clockwise.

| | Track2 | Singapore |
|---|---|---|
| length | 228.4 m | 198.9 m |
| min clearance between non-neighbouring centerline points (must be > 3.8 m) | 5.97 m | 6.00 m |
| tightest corner radius | 2.5 m (hairpin) | 1.6 m (T13 hairpin) |
| longest straight | 55.3 m | 32 m |
| top of the grip-limited speed profile (mu = 1.05) | 20.0 m/s (capped) | 15.0 m/s |
| mean of the speed profile | 10.4 m/s | 7.6 m/s |

Corners (s = distance from the start line):

| s (m) | corner |
|---|---|
| 1-56 | main straight, 55 m |
| 56-78 | T1/T2 fast left sweeper: two 45 deg arcs, r = 10 m, 5.7 m apart |
| 91-108 | T3, T4: two 90 deg lefts, r = 3 m |
| 115-124 | T5/T6 hairpin: two 90 deg rights, r = 2.5 m, legs 6 m apart |
| 139-145 | T7 90 deg left, r = 3.5 m, onto the 26 m back straight |
| 171-192 | T8 35 deg left (r = 6 m), T9 27 deg left kink (r = 5 m) |
| 216-223 | T10 100 deg left (r = 3.9 m) onto the main straight |

So Track2 is faster and more open than Singapore (long straights, a high-speed sweeper, heavy braking zones), with one
tight hairpin, and it has a right-hander pair (the hairpin) where Singapore is mostly lefts.

Regenerate: `python -m racing.track_gen` (all tracks) or `python -m racing.track_gen Track2`. The Singapore output is
byte-identical to the committed files.

## Using it

- Environment: `EnvConfig(map_name="Track2")` (default `"Singapore"`, unchanged behaviour). The map is used by the
  simulator, the centerline (progress, laps, opponents, shield map walls) and `racing/render.py`.
- Race evaluation: `python -m racing.evaluate --map Track2 ...` (default `Singapore`). The job tuple of `run_race`
  takes an optional 9th element, the map name; each race record and the output json carry `"map"`.
- Videos: `python -m racing.render --map Track2 ...`.
- Solo check of the classical controllers: `python -m scripts.probe_track2 [map] [tire]`.
- `EnvConfig.hotspot_s` (opt-in fine-tuning) is in Singapore coordinates; do not use it with Track2.

## Solo check (`scripts/probe_track2.py`, 3 laps, 25 Hz, no opponents)

| controller | tyres | result | lap times (s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PID+APF | saturated_v2 / saturated / linear | 3/3 laps, no contact, max offset 0.20 m | 33.16, 32.80, 32.80 |
| Pure Pursuit | linear (stock) | 3/3 laps | 30.76, 30.24, 30.24 |
| Pure Pursuit | saturated_v2 and saturated | wall on lap 3 (2 laps done) | 30.76, 30.24 |

Pure Pursuit (0.85 of the profile) reaches 15.9 m/s on the main straight and spins under braking at its end
(s ~ 55 m, body slip up to 13 deg) with the friction-circle tyres. Singapore never gets it above 12.2 m/s. The
classical baseline is less robust on long straights; this is a property of the controller, not a track defect.

## Smoke test: race evaluation on Track2

```
python -m racing.evaluate --map Track2 --controllers pid_apf "ppo_apf_rec@s0" \
    --models "ppo_apf_rec@s0=runs/ppo_shield_s0/model_stage3.zip" --races 5 --laps 3 --procs 2 \
    --tire saturated_v2 --out results/track2_smoke.json
```

5 races x 3 laps per condition, 3 opponents, seeds 10000-10004. `ppo_apf_rec@s0` is the final Singapore policy
(seed 0, stage 3) with the shield, deadlock escape and stall recovery.

| condition | controller | finished | collisions / 10 laps | clean-lap mean (best) s | first lap s | passes / race (ok / attempts) | shield active | grip > 90% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nominal | PID+APF | 4/5 | 0.80 (1 car contact) | 38.40 (32.80) | 40.30 | 1.00 (5/8) | 94.5% | 1.2% |
| nominal | PPO+shield (ours) | 5/5 | 0.00 | 37.84 (33.60) | 39.83 | 1.80 (9/13) | 1.8% | 12.5% |
| randomised | PID+APF | 4/5 | 0.81 (1 car contact) | 37.28 (32.00) | 41.13 | 1.60 (8/9) | 95.3% | 0.3% |
| randomised | PPO+shield (ours) | 5/5 | 0.00 | 38.05 (33.44) | 39.33 | 0.40 (2/6) | 2.2% | 5.2% |

No stuck races, no wall contacts, 0% of time above 15 deg slip for both. The PPO policy used the stall recovery in 3
of 10 races (once each).

Reading (5 races only, so indicative): the policy and shield transfer without retraining — every race finished
without contact — but the lap-time advantage seen on Singapore (34.7 s vs 42.8 s for PID+APF) is gone: on Track2 the
clean-lap means are within 1 s of PID+APF and best laps are slightly slower. Laps vary with traffic (33-44 s), since
opponents are hard to pass on this layout. A larger study (20+ races, more seeds) is needed before claiming anything
beyond "no collapse".

## Full evaluation

20 races x 5 laps per controller: see `docs/experiments.md` E28 and `results/eval_track2.json`. The 5-race smoke
test above was too pessimistic about the learned policies (seed 0: 16/20 finished, 36.7 s vs PID+APF 39.3 s).
